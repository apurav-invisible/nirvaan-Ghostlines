The City Paints Over Everything

Urban Memory September 2026

Every wall in every city is a document. Paint layers accumulate over decades — brand names, phone numbers, business hours, political slogans. When the new layer goes on, the old one doesn't vanish. It retreats. It bleeds through. It becomes a ghost.

"The repaint is never absolute. Given five monsoon seasons, the original wall will always state its name."
